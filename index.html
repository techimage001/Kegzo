How does Kegzo work?

You answer four quick safety questions, learn to find the right muscle, then follow one short guided session a day. The coach counts every squeeze, hold and release with you, and the programme gets harder each week as you move from lying down to walking.

1. Safety check

Four questions decide your starting track. If your pelvic floor sounds tense, you start on the Relax track instead of squeezing harder.

2. Find the muscle

A guided check makes sure you are training the pelvic floor, not your bum, belly or thighs.

3. Train and level up

Daily sessions with a live animated coach, XP, streaks with a weekly rest-day shield, and a boss challenge every week.

What is in the 28-day programme?

Each week adds a harder position and new exercises. Week 1 is lying down, week 2 seated, week 3 standing and week 4 walking. Every session mixes strength holds, quick flicks, control work and reverse kegels for a full release.

Why train your pelvic floor?

Your pelvic floor is a group of muscles like any other: it can be trained for strength, speed, stamina, control and the ability to relax. Kegzo gives that training a clear structure, a coach to follow and numbers that show your progress.
