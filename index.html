How does Kegzo work?

Three simple steps. Answer a few quick questions to get your plan, press Start once a day and follow the coach, then level up as you go. Each week moves to a new position, from lying down to walking.

  1. 1
    Get your plan

    Answer 9 quick taps about your goal, your time and how things feel. Kegzo builds your 28-day path.

  2. 2
    Press Start once a day

    Follow the coach's voice and the video. He counts every squeeze, hold and release for you.

  3. 3
    Level up

    Earn XP, keep your streak, unlock badges and watch your progress grow on your dashboard.

What is in the 28-day programme?

Each week adds a harder position and new exercises. Week 1 is lying down, week 2 seated, week 3 standing and week 4 walking. Every session mixes strength holds, quick flicks, control work and reverse kegels for a full release.

Week 1: Lying downFind and lift, Power holds, Quick flicks, Reverse kegel, Endurance hold
Week 2: SeatedPower holds, The elevator, Quick flicks, Pyramid, Reverse kegel
Week 3: StandingPower holds, Wave squeezes, The elevator, Endurance hold, Reverse kegel
Week 4: WalkingWalking holds, Power holds, Wave squeezes, Pyramid, Reverse kegel

Why train your pelvic floor?

Your pelvic floor is a group of muscles like any other: it can be trained for strength, speed, stamina, control and the ability to relax. Kegzo gives that training a clear structure, a coach to follow and numbers that show your progress.

Frequently asked questions

What is Kegzo?

Kegzo is a 28-day kegel training programme for men, built like a game. Each day you follow a short guided session with a live animated coach, earn XP, keep a streak and track real progress with a weekly max-hold test.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start straight away on any phone. Signing up lets your progress follow you to other devices.

How long does a session take?

Most daily sessions take around 6 to 8 minutes, including short rests.

Is my training private?

Yes. Your sessions and test results are stored in your browser on your device. Nothing about your training is sent to us. If you sign up, we store only your email address.

Add Kegzo to your home screen

You opened Kegzo inside another app. First tap ⋯ or the compass icon and choose Open in Safari, then follow the steps below.

  1. In Safari, tap ⋯ (bottom right) and then Share . In Chrome, tap the Share icon in the address bar.
  2. Tap View More if you need to, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App on and tap Add. Kegzo appears on your home screen.
