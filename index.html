Why kegels

Your pelvic floor includes the muscles at the base of the penis that are active during an erection. In a UK randomised trial, men with erection problems who did guided pelvic floor exercises for 3 months scored significantly better than men given lifestyle advice only. Read the study

Erection problems can be an early sign of heart or circulation issues, so a check-up with a doctor is always worth it. Kegzo is training, not treatment.
