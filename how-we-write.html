How we write and check our guides

Every Kegzo guide is written in plain English, based on published research and recognised physiotherapy practice, and checked so it never promises results we cannot back up. Kegzo is the publisher of all content on this site.

Our sources

We draw on peer-reviewed research, and on widely used pelvic floor physiotherapy guidance. Where we refer to a study, we link to it.

Our rules

  • Answer the question first, in plain words.
  • Describe what exercises train, never promise what they will cure.
  • Always include when to stop and when to see a professional.
  • Never name or copy other apps.
  • Correct mistakes quickly when we find them.
