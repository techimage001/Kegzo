Kegels and erections: what the research says

The pelvic floor includes the muscles at the base of the penis that are active during an erection. In a UK randomised controlled trial, men with erection problems who did guided pelvic floor exercises for three months had significantly better erectile function scores than men given lifestyle advice only. Kegels are training, not treatment, so a check-up with a doctor is still worth it.

Which muscles are involved?

Two pelvic floor muscles sit at the base of the penis: the bulbocavernosus and the ischiocavernosus. They are active during an erection, and like other muscles they respond to training.

What did the trial find?

The study, led by physiotherapist Grace Dorey and published in BJU International, recruited 55 men aged over 20 who had experienced erection problems for at least six months. One group was taught pelvic floor exercises by a physiotherapist, with biofeedback and lifestyle advice. The other group received lifestyle advice only.

After three months, erectile function in the exercise group was significantly better than in the advice-only group. When the advice-only group then did the exercises too, they also improved. Read the study abstract.

What this does not mean

One trial with a small number of men is encouraging but not a guarantee. The men in the study were taught by a physiotherapist, which an app cannot replace. Results vary, and some causes of erection problems are not muscular at all.

Why see a doctor too?

Erection problems can be an early sign of heart disease, diabetes or circulation problems. A check-up with a GP is worth it, especially if the problem is new. You can train your pelvic floor alongside any advice you are given.

Frequently asked questions

How long did the men in the trial train?

Three months of exercises taught by a physiotherapist, with progress measured at the end.

Can kegels make things worse?

For men whose pelvic floor is already too tight, more squeezing can add tension. That is why Kegzo starts with a short safety check and includes reverse kegels in every session.
