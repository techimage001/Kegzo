Kegel positions: why you progress from lying to walking

Kegels start lying down because gravity helps and the muscle is easiest to find. As control improves, you progress to seated, then standing, where the muscle works against gravity, and finally walking, where it has to keep working while the body moves. Kegzo moves you up one position each week.

Week 1: lying down

On your back, knees bent, feet flat. The easiest position to learn the feeling and train clean holds.

Week 2: seated

Upright on a firm chair, feet flat, weight across both sit bones. You can feel the lift against the chair.

Week 3: standing

Tall, feet hip-width apart, knees soft. Harder because the muscle lifts against gravity, and closer to everyday life.

Week 4: walking

An easy pace while holding a steady squeeze for a few steps. This trains the pelvic floor to work while the rest of your body is busy.

Should I go back to easier positions?

Yes, whenever technique slips. There is no shame in a lying-down session after a long day.

Frequently asked questions

Can I do kegels while driving?

Seated kegels are possible anywhere, but keep your full attention on the road. Practise at home first.

Why not start standing?

Starting lying down makes it easier to find the right muscle without other muscles helping.
