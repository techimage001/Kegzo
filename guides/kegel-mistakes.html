Common kegel mistakes men make, and how to fix them

The most common kegel mistakes are squeezing the glutes, belly or thighs instead of the pelvic floor, holding your breath, squeezing too hard, never fully relaxing between reps and skipping training for days at a time. Each one has a simple fix: lighter effort, steady breathing and a full release every rep.

Mistake 1: using the glutes

If your bum lifts or tightens, the glutes are doing the work. Fix: lie down, use half effort and focus on the area between your legs, not behind.

Mistake 2: holding your breath

Breath-holding raises pressure inside the belly and pushes down on the pelvic floor. Fix: breathe out gently as you squeeze and keep breathing during holds.

Mistake 3: squeezing as hard as possible

Maximum effort recruits everything around the pelvic floor. Fix: start at half effort and build up once the technique is clean.

Mistake 4: never letting go

A rep is a squeeze and a full release. Staying partly tense trains tension, not control. Fix: finish every rep with a complete release and add reverse kegels.

Mistake 5: training in bursts

Long sessions once a week build less than short sessions most days. Fix: keep sessions short and regular, and use a streak to stay consistent.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if I am squeezing too hard?

If your face, shoulders, bum or belly tense up, ease off.

Is it a mistake to only do long holds?

Yes. Mixing holds, flicks, control work and release gives better all-round training.
