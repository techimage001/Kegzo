Terms of use

Kegzo is a training game that provides general fitness information, not medical advice. By using it you agree to train sensibly, stop if anything hurts, and speak to a qualified professional about any health concern.

Not medical advice

Kegzo does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Nothing on this site replaces advice from a doctor or physiotherapist. If you have pain, recent surgery or ongoing symptoms, get professional advice before starting.

Using Kegzo

  • You must be 18 or over.
  • Stop any exercise that causes pain or discomfort.
  • Kegzo is currently free. Features may change over time.
  • Don't misuse the service, for example by sending automated sign-ups or messages.

No guarantees

We work hard to keep Kegzo accurate and available, but we provide it as it is, without guarantees of results or uninterrupted access.

Changes

We may update these terms. The current version is always on this page.
