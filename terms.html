Add Kegzo to your home screen

You opened Kegzo inside another app. First tap ⋯ or the compass icon and choose Open in Safari, then follow the steps below.

  1. In Safari, tap ⋯ (bottom right) and then Share . In Chrome, tap the Share icon in the address bar.
  2. Tap View More if you need to, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App on and tap Add. Kegzo appears on your home screen.
