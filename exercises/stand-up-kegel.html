Stand-up kegel: how to do it

This bonus exercise links a kegel to one of the most common movements of the day: getting up from a chair. Squeezing just before you stand trains the pelvic floor to switch on when the pressure in your belly rises.

Try Stand-up kegel with the coachCurrently free. Start straight away on any phone.

How do you do stand-up kegel?

  1. Sit near the front of a sturdy chair, feet flat, arms crossed over your chest.
  2. Squeeze and lift your pelvic floor.
  3. Keep the squeeze on as you stand up, pause, then sit back down slowly.
  4. Let the squeeze go fully once you are seated.

What should you feel?

The lift inside staying on as you rise and lower.

What should you avoid?

  • Dropping into the chair
  • Letting the squeeze fade as you stand
  • Holding your breath
Coach tip: Squeeze first, then move. This is a habit worth using every time you get up.

When does it appear in the programme?

It is a bonus exercise you can try any time, practised seated. Sets: 6 sit-to-stands with a kegel.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use my hands?

Cross your arms if you can. If you need your hands to stand, that is fine.

How often can I use this?

Any time you get up from a chair. It is an easy way to practise all day.
