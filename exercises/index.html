Kegel exercises for men

These ten kegel exercises train every part of the pelvic floor: finding the muscle, strength, speed, stamina, control and release. In the Kegzo programme they unlock week by week, from lying down to walking.

Week 1: Lying down

Find and liftLearn to find and lift the right muscle before anything else.Power holdsStrong squeezes held for up to 10 seconds to build strength.Quick flicksFast, strong squeezes with a full release to train speed.Reverse kegelBreathe in and let the pelvic floor soften and drop.Endurance holdA gentle squeeze held for longer to build stamina.

Week 2: Seated

The elevatorLift in three stages, then lower one floor at a time.PyramidHolds that build up to 8 seconds and back down.

Week 3: Standing

Wave squeezesTwo quick pulses, then a strong hold.

Week 4: Walking

Walking holdsHold the squeeze while you walk at an easy pace.
