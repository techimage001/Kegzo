Side-lying release: how to do it

This bonus exercise is the gentlest position in Kegzo. Lying on your side takes all the effort away, so you can focus on breathing in slowly and letting the pelvic floor soften and drop, then return to rest as you breathe out.

Try Side-lying release with the coachCurrently free. Start straight away on any phone.

How do you do side-lying release?

  1. Lie on your side with your knees bent and stacked.
  2. Rest your head on your arm and let your body go heavy.
  3. Breathe in slowly and let the pelvic floor soften and drop.
  4. Breathe out slowly and let it return to rest, without squeezing.

What should you feel?

A gentle widening low down as you breathe in, and calm as you breathe out.

What should you avoid?

  • Pushing or straining down
  • Holding tension in your jaw or shoulders
  • Rushing the breath
Coach tip: This is the easiest position of all. Use it on days you feel tense or tired.

When does it appear in the programme?

It is a bonus exercise you can try any time, practised side-lying. Sets: 6 slow breaths lying on your side.

Frequently asked questions

Which side should I lie on?

Either side is fine. Choose whichever is more comfortable.

When is this useful?

On days you feel tense or tired, or as a calm way to finish a session.
