Pyramid: how to do it

The pyramid tests strength and stamina in one set. Each hold gets longer up to 8 seconds, then shorter again, with a rest after each one as long as the hold.

Try Pyramid with the coachCurrently free. Start straight away on any phone.

How do you do pyramid?

  1. Squeeze and hold for 2 seconds, then rest.
  2. Each hold gets longer: 4, 6, then 8 seconds.
  3. Then come back down: 6, 4, 2.

What should you feel?

Your strength being tested as the holds get longer.

What should you avoid?

  • Letting the squeeze fade mid-hold
  • Skipping the rest
  • Holding your breath
Coach tip: If you cannot hold 8 seconds yet, relax early and keep going.

When does it appear in the programme?

It unlocks in week 2 of the programme and is first practised seated. Sets: Holds of 2, 4, 6, 8, 6, 4, 2 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

What if I cannot reach 8 seconds?

Relax when the squeeze fades and carry on with the next hold. Your ceiling rises with practice.

When does the pyramid unlock?

In week 2 of the programme, once the basics are in place.
