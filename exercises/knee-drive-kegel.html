Knee drive kegel: how to do it

This bonus exercise pairs a slow knee drive with a kegel. You squeeze as the knee comes in and let go as the leg goes back, which links the pelvic floor to movement and trains a full release after every squeeze.

Try Knee drive kegel with the coachStart straight away on any phone.

How do you do knee drive kegel?

  1. Start in a high plank, body in a straight line.
  2. Slowly draw one knee towards your chest and squeeze your pelvic floor as it comes in.
  3. Hold the squeeze for two seconds.
  4. Take the leg back and let the squeeze go. Switch legs each time.

What should you feel?

A lift inside as the knee comes in, and a full release as the leg goes back.

What should you avoid?

  • Rushing like a cardio move
  • Letting your hips bounce
  • Holding your breath
Coach tip: Slow is the point. One knee, one squeeze, one release.

When does it appear in the programme?

It is a bonus exercise you can try any time, practised plank. Sets: 8 slow knee drives with a kegel.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a cardio move?

No. Go slowly. Each knee drive is one controlled squeeze and one full release.

Can I do it with my knees down?

Yes. From all fours, slide one knee forward a little and squeeze as it moves.
