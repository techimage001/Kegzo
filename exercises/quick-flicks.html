Quick flicks: how to do it

Your pelvic floor needs speed as well as strength. Quick flicks train the fast-reacting fibres with short, sharp squeezes, and the full release between each one keeps the muscle from staying tense.

Try Quick flicks with the coachCurrently free. Start straight away on any phone.

How do you do quick flicks?

  1. Squeeze hard and fast, then let go straight away.
  2. Each flick is a full squeeze and a full release.
  3. Keep a steady rhythm and stay relaxed everywhere else.

What should you feel?

Quick pulses deep in the pelvis.

What should you avoid?

  • Half squeezes that never fully release
  • Bouncing your whole body
  • Rushing so the squeezes get weak
Coach tip: Quality first. Slow down if the squeezes get weaker.

When does it appear in the programme?

It unlocks in week 1 of the programme and is first practised lying down. Sets: 2 sets of 10 to 15 flicks.

Frequently asked questions

How fast should flicks be?

About one squeeze and one release every second or two. Slow down if the squeezes get weaker.

Do I need to fully relax between flicks?

Yes. A full release every time is half of the exercise.
