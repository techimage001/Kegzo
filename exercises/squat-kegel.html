Squat kegel: how to do it

This bonus exercise keeps a kegel switched on through a small squat. It trains the pelvic floor to hold while your legs and hips move, the way it needs to when you bend, lift or get up from a chair.

Try Squat kegel with the coachCurrently free. Start straight away on any phone.

How do you do squat kegel?

  1. Stand tall, feet hip-width apart, arms reaching forward.
  2. Squeeze and lift your pelvic floor.
  3. Keep the squeeze on as you bend your knees into a small squat, then stand back up.
  4. Let the squeeze go fully at the top and rest.

What should you feel?

The lift inside staying on while your legs do the work.

What should you avoid?

  • Squatting too low
  • Letting the squeeze fade as you move
  • Holding your breath
Coach tip: Small squats are enough. The squeeze is what you are training.

When does it appear in the programme?

It is a bonus exercise you can try any time, practised standing. Sets: 6 small squats with a kegel.

Frequently asked questions

How low should I squat?

Only a little, about a quarter of the way down. Stop well before it feels like hard work.

What if I lose the squeeze?

Squat less deep or more slowly, and keep breathing.
