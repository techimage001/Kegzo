Power holds: how to do it

Power holds are the core strength exercise of most pelvic floor programmes. A firm squeeze held for several seconds, followed by full relaxation and an equal rest, builds the strength of the muscles at the base of the penis.

Try Power holds with the coachCurrently free. Start straight away on any phone.

How do you do power holds?

  1. Squeeze as hard as you can while still breathing normally.
  2. Hold the squeeze for the full count. If it fades, relax and reset.
  3. Let go completely, then rest as long as you held.

What should you feel?

A strong, steady lift that you can keep without shaking.

What should you avoid?

  • Squeezing your buttocks to help
  • Holding your breath to get a stronger squeeze
  • Cutting the rest short
Coach tip: Holds grow by a second each level, up to 10 seconds.

When does it appear in the programme?

It unlocks in week 1 of the programme and is first practised lying down. Sets: 6 holds of 5 to 10 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

How long should each hold be?

Start with what you can hold cleanly, often 4 or 5 seconds, and add a second as you level up, up to 10 seconds.

What if the squeeze fades before the count ends?

Relax, rest and start the next one. A clean shorter hold beats a long weak one.
