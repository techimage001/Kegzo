Endurance hold: how to do it

Endurance holds train the muscle to keep working at a moderate level for longer. The squeeze is light, around half strength, and the goal is to keep it steady while breathing normally.

Try Endurance hold with the coachCurrently free. Start straight away on any phone.

How do you do endurance hold?

  1. Squeeze at about half strength.
  2. Hold that steady squeeze for the whole count while breathing normally.
  3. Relax fully at the end.

What should you feel?

A light, steady lift you could keep while talking.

What should you avoid?

  • Starting too hard and fading
  • Holding your breath
  • Tensing your shoulders
Coach tip: Think of it as a gentle hold, not a maximum effort.

When does it appear in the programme?

It unlocks in week 1 of the programme and is first practised lying down. Sets: 2 long holds at half strength.

Frequently asked questions

How strong should the squeeze be?

About half your maximum. You should be able to talk while holding it.

Is it normal for the hold to fade?

Yes, at first. Relax fully and try again next session. Holds get longer as you level up.
