Lunge kegel: how to do it

This advanced bonus exercise keeps a kegel switched on through a lunge. It is one of the harder positions for the pelvic floor to hold, so it is a good test of strength and control once the earlier exercises feel easy.

Try Lunge kegel with the coachCurrently free. Start straight away on any phone.

How do you do lunge kegel?

  1. Stand tall with your hands on your hips.
  2. Squeeze and lift your pelvic floor.
  3. Step forward and lower into a lunge, keeping the squeeze on.
  4. Push back up to standing, then let the squeeze go. Switch legs each time.

What should you feel?

The lift inside holding steady while your legs do the hard work.

What should you avoid?

  • Going too low
  • Letting your front knee pass your toes
  • Losing the squeeze as you push back up
Coach tip: This is the hardest one. Make the lunge small until the squeeze stays on.

When does it appear in the programme?

It is a bonus exercise you can try any time, practised standing. Sets: 6 lunges with a kegel.

Frequently asked questions

How deep should I lunge?

Only as deep as you can go while keeping the squeeze. Small is fine.

Should I switch legs?

Yes. Step forward with a different leg each time.
